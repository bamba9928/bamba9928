I'm Bamba, a full-stack engineer based in Dakar and the founder of **[Horus Global Service](https://horuservices.cloud/)**. I turn demanding business workflows into fast, secure web, mobile and desktop applications, mostly for **insurance, real-estate and service companies** in Senegal and West Africa.

- **Backend architecture.** Modular Django 6 / DRF APIs, JWT auth, role-based access, multi-tenant data isolation, OpenAPI docs, and PostgreSQL queries kept free of N+1s.
- **Product interfaces.** Next.js and React on the web, Expo on mobile, Tauri and Rust on the desktop.
- **Integrations.** Partner insurance APIs, mobile money and card payments (Wave, Orange Money, Stripe), webhooks.
- **Security by default.** OWASP Top 10, hardened Linux servers, audits that end in a prioritized action plan, and a pentest lab of my own.
- **Delivery.** Docker, Nginx or Caddy, CI, monitoring, and a documented hand-over.

> **Now:** building **Horus Digital Assurances**, where brokers quote, collect payment and issue the motor-insurance certificate from their phone.
